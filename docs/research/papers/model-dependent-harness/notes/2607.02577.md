# Paper note: Benchmarking the Benchmarks

Final disposition: experiment-candidate

The paper audits tool-calling evaluation and reports disagreements between
benchmark evaluators and expert review, plus material variance in repeated LLM
judging. Its key transfer is an evaluator audit: task outcome, tool correctness,
side effects, evidence validity, and process quality must remain distinct.

Transfer: label the same swe-term trajectories with deterministic checks, two
human passes, and repeated model-judge calls. Report disagreement per dimension,
not a collapsed average. The paper's benchmark-specific corrected components are
not imported.

Sources: <https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.02577>,
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02577>.
